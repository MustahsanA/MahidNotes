Grade 1 – FA-2 Project  
**Topic: Save Water, Share Water**

- **My Water Diary**  
    Project Type: Lapbook  
    Instructions:  
    Students will make a Lapbook showing how we use, save and share water in their assigned place. Each child will prepare the lapbook based on the topic allotted to their roll number.  
    Topic Allotment:  
    Roll Numbers 1–5 – Water at Home  
    Roll Numbers 6–10 – Water at School  
    Roll Numbers 11–16 – Water for Animals & Plants  
    Roll Numbers 17–22 – Water in the Masjid / Park  
    What to include in the Lapbook (4 pages only)  
    1. Where do we get water?
- Tap, well, rain, river (draw / paste)  
    2. How do we use water?
- Drinking, wudu, washing hands, bathing, watering plants  
    3. How do we SAVE water?
- Close tap, use small mug for wudu, don't waste  
    4. How do we SHARE water?
- Share bottle with friend, keep bowl for birds, water for plants

Materials Required:

- A3 or coloured chart papers
- Glue, scissors, crayons, sketch pens
- Old magazine / newspaper cutouts of water, animals, plants
- 1 small blue paper water-drop cutout
- Stickers or coloured paper for decoration
- Add one small sentence under each picture, e.g. "I close the tap."
